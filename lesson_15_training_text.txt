Mia lives in a small house near a green park. She has a little garden behind the house. Every morning, Mia walks to the garden with her dog, Ben. Ben likes to sit in the sun and watch the birds.

One day, Mia finds a paper bag with some seeds. Her friend Sam comes to help her plant them. They dig small holes in the soft soil and put one seed in each hole. Then they cover the seeds and give the garden water.

Every day, Mia and Sam look at the garden. They give the seeds water, pull out the weeds, and keep the path clean. Ben walks beside them, but he stays out of the garden beds. The friends want the seeds to grow into strong plants.

At first, nothing changes. Mia asks Sam, "Will the seeds grow?" Sam says, "Plants need time, sun, and water." The friends wait and take care of the garden. Ben waits with them and rests in the shade.

After some days, a small green shoot comes out of the soil. Mia calls Sam, and they look at the new plant. They see two little leaves in the warm sun. Ben sniffs the plant, then sits by the path and watches it grow.

The next week, soft rain falls on the park and the garden. Mia does not water the soil that day. When the rain stops, Sam helps her move a small stone away from the plant. The leaves look green and bright after the rain.

Soon, more plants grow in the garden. Some plants have long leaves, and some have small yellow flowers. Mia and Sam put a low fence around the garden. Ben likes the flowers, but he does not step over the fence.

One morning, a little bird lands near the garden. It looks for seeds on the path. Ben watches the bird from beside Mia. The bird flies to a tree, and Ben sits quietly in the sun.

On Saturday, Mia and Sam invite their friends to the park. They show everyone the plants and the flowers. The friends bring apples, bread, and fresh water to share. Ben sits under the table and waits for a small piece of apple.

Mia gives each friend a few seeds to take home. She says, "Give the seeds sun, water, and time." Sam helps clean the table, and the friends put every cup away. They promise to visit the garden again next week.

That evening, Mia walks home with Ben. She looks back at the small garden beside the park. The plants move softly in the cool wind. Mia is happy because she and her friends helped the seeds grow. In the morning, they will visit the garden again.
